Procurement analytics · Indonesian upstream oil & gas operator · PO lines 2021–2025

A text classifier assigned every PO line to a 50-category taxonomy (UNSPSC-aligned). Duplicate vendor records were merged under one tax ID. The result is a spend cube by category, vendor, site and year.

    Method: Python (pandas, scikit-learn). TF-IDF character and word n-grams with a calibrated linear SVM; confidence threshold 0.20; lines below the threshold keep the buyer label or go to a manual review queue. Spend in USD at monthly IDR/USD rates. Data shown is a synthetic replica of the confidential client extract, matching its structure, volumes and data-quality issues.
